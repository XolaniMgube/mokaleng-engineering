About Mokaleng

Mokaleng SMP is an engineering consulting and construction entity that has been in operation since 2015. The company provides specialised engineering solutions and value-added services within the structural, mechanical, piping, metallurgical, welding, and quality risk-based inspection sectors.

Our expertise extends across the fabrication, installation, maintenance, and repair of piping systems, structural steelwork, pressure equipment, and mechanical components within the petrochemical, power generation, and utilities industries.

Backed by a highly experienced and qualified team with more than 100 years of combined industry expertise, Mokaleng is committed to delivering safe, cost-effective, and high-quality engineering solutions aligned with industry standards and client requirements.
